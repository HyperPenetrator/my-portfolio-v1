### 📂 Featured Projects

#### 🌊 [Project LUIT](https://github.com/HyperPenetrator/LUIT-CleanWater)
> **Autonomous River Cleaning & Environmental Intelligence Platform**
*   **Overview:** An AI-powered platform designed to tackle plastic waste and debris in the Brahmaputra River.
*   **Technology:** Features a custom **YOLOv8** computer vision model optimized for detecting and classifying floating debris in varying aquatic environments and lighting conditions.
*   **Impact:** Aims to automate robotic collection systems and provide real-time debris maps to environmental conservation teams.

#### 🚨 [Project My_Safety](https://github.com/HyperPenetrator/My_Safety)
> **Voice-Activated Campus Emergency Support System** *(Developed with Team CodeCraft)*
*   **Overview:** An instant-response safety system designed to protect students and campus staff.
*   **Technology:** Integrated edge-voice trigger detection, instant location broadcasting, and cellular-connected alerts.
*   **Impact:** Reduces emergency response times on campus by utilizing ambient audio trigger-words to activate help protocols without physical phone interaction.
